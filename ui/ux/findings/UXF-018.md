"change.m…", its executor, holder and reason end in an ellipsis, and the collapsed rail (a fixed
40 px column) lies over the card's right part. Once the page scrolls (the fix lets it), nothing keeps
the state and the step in view. Every scenario. Cost: M.

(action and who runs or holds it, or what a stopped run was doing); the cards wrap instead of cutting.
